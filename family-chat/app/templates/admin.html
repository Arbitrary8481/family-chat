Adding a channel is self-service — anyone can do it from the chat's own Settings (✏️) → Channels tab, into any category that already exists. Dragging a channel (admin/owner only) reorders it, or drops it into a different category entirely — grab it by the ⠿ handle. Deleting one just hides it from the sidebar, its messages aren't deleted, and it reappears if a channel with the same name is added again later. At least one channel must always exist.
